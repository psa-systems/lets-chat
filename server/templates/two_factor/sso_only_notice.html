Two-factor authentication

Your account signs in through an identity provider that handles authentication (including any second factor) for you. Let's Chat doesn't run its own TOTP challenge in that case, so the local 2FA enrollment form is disabled.

To set up two-factor authentication, configure it in your identity provider's account settings.
